What it catches

The three ways a serial loses its audience

They rarely leave over a bad line. They leave over a broken world, a promise you dropped, or an ending that felt cheap.

Canon breaks

Every established fact — timelines, injuries, who knows what and when — checked against the new episode. Flagged with the earlier line it contradicts.

Unresolved emotional promises

The setup in episode 4 that the audience is still holding. CanonPulse tracks each open promise, how long it's been open, and how hot it still runs.

Unfair cliffhangers

A fair cliffhanger withholds an outcome. An unfair one withholds information the listener needed. CanonPulse scores which one you wrote.

A canon bible that writes itself

Characters, places, rules, and continuity built from your back catalog on first ingest. No spreadsheet to maintain, no showrunner memory to trust.

Script or finished audio

Feed it a draft in Fountain, Final Draft, or plain text — or the mixed episode. Improvised and ad-libbed lines get caught the same way.

Unreleased work stays unreleased

Private workspaces, no training on your material, SSO and per-season access for writer rooms. Your season finale is not our dataset.
